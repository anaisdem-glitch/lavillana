Five minutes' walk from the beach and at the edge of the state forest, La Villana is a modern, warm and tastefully decorated single-storey house. Large picture windows, an enclosed private garden and an 80 m² wooden terrace: a bright home that is pleasant all year round – including spring, autumn and winter, thanks to its pellet stove.
